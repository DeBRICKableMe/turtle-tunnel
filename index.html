Are turtles in our area running into trouble with roads and habitat?

We're a local student team trying to find out. Roads, development, and changing habitat can affect how turtles move through a community — but we don't yet know how much that's happening here. Your sightings are the data we need to find out.

What we're investigating

Whether turtles in our local area are encountering problems related to roads, habitat, or getting safely from place to place. We're studying the question before assuming the answer.

Why we need your help

Our team can't be everywhere. Community sightings — in yards, near roads, at ponds and creeks — are how we build a real, local picture of where turtles are and what they're running into.

How you can help

If you've seen a turtle in Licking County in the last 18 months, it takes about a minute to tell us about it. Every report — even a simple sighting — helps.

Our goal: 100 local reports

We're aiming to collect at least 100 turtle observations from around Licking County. This isn't a finish line for the research — it's the amount of data we think we need to start seeing real patterns in where and how turtles are moving through our community.
