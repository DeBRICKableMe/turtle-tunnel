Have you seen a turtle in Licking County?

We're a local student team investigating where turtles are being seen in our community and what they're encountering along roads.

If you've seen a turtle or any other animal on the road in the last 18 months, tell us about it. Your observation could help us see patterns that we wouldn't be able to find on our own.

What we're trying to find out

We're looking at where turtles are being seen around Licking County, especially when they're on or near roads. We're interested in things like turtles crossing roads, along roadsides, or near ditches.

Why your report matters

We can't be everywhere at once. A turtle you saw while driving, walking, or exploring your neighborhood could be one small piece of a much bigger picture.

How you can help

If you've seen a turtle in Licking County within the last 18 months, we'd love to hear about it. It takes about a minute to submit a report, and every observation helps us build our dataset.

Our Goal: 100 reports

We're working toward 100 turtle reports from around Licking County. Once we reach that goal, we'll take a closer look at the data and see what patterns emerge, where turtles are being seen, what they're doing, and how often roads are part of the picture.
